Host simulations ran the actual Robot.cpp and Logic.h with mocked Arduino I/O
and synthetic sensor data, using address and undefined-behaviour sanitizers.
Checks passed for:
  - recognition of six distinguishable synthetic colour samples;
  - rejection of incomplete calibration, outliers and ambiguous colours;
  - red/green/orange turn actions;
  - pink/blue turn-drive-turn ordering, with no second strip;
  - white finish with motors stopped and no subsequent movement;
  - uncertain colour stops rather than guessing a turn;
  - abort during a timed drive, a double turn and grid travel;
  - stop text split across idle/busy serial reads;
  - correct steering-correction signs and no pull towards missing walls;
  - invalid IR and both-sides-too-close stop conditions;
  - a high off-baseline in low ambient IR remaining valid;
  - wrong-sign IR response being rejected;
  - black-strip rearming only after continuous clear readings;
  - calibration persistence and corrupt EEPROM rejection;
  - motor disarming and rejection of invalid pulse durations;
  - rejection of calibration commands while a race is running.

Not checked on hardware:
  - actual wiring, electrical correctness or motor polarities;
  - upload/USB driver compatibility on your computer;
  - real sensor values, optical settling, ADC noise or ambient-light robustness;
  - stopping distances, accurate angles, speed or grid travel;
  - serial communication latency on the actual board;
  - memory stack high-water mark during an actual race;
  - collision avoidance and timing score in any physical maze.
